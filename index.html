About us

A school built around the child, not the syllabus.

We believe good schooling is equal parts learning and character. Our classrooms are small enough for teachers to notice who is struggling and who is ready for more, and our values of honesty, discipline and respect are practised every day, not just printed on a wall.

Parents are partners here: regular meetings, open doors and clear communication keep home and school moving in the same direction.
